A life, kept chapter by chapter.

A normal CRM overwrites you. You change jobs, it changes the job field, and the old you is gone. A life CRM keeps every chapter — what changed, when it changed, and why — so you can ask about a life at any point in time, not just its latest version.

“I don’t sell a memory that remembers better. I sell the record that can’t be rewritten, so when an agent acts you can prove what it believed, when, and why.”

How to read this demo. This is a scripted demo, primed for presentation — that priming is disclosed, not hidden. Ada Okafor here is a demo persona, not a real customer. The story below is seeded. The chapter pull-apart is a small demo parser: it counts independent supporting sentences in the story, it does not pretend to be the full Overseer engine. The record it illustrates is append-only: entries are added, never edited. The full real record is linked at the bottom.

Step 1 — Seed the story

Edit this story if you like — remove a sentence and its topic loses support. Then pull it apart.

Step 2 — Chapters pulled from the story

One tangled life, eleven threads. Support is counted, not judged: one independent supporting sentence moves a chapter one shade darker, on a 0–9 greyscale. Shades 8–9 are the black band — a chapter gets filed. Below that, it stays grey.

Press “Pull it apart” to see the chapters.

Step 3 — Ask about a life, at a point in time

Now chat. Ask what Ada’s life looked like on Monday, or every job she has ever had and why each one changed — the record answers from the chapters, not just the latest version.
